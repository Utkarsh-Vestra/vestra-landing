Meet the Founder

Founder & CEO

Utkarsh Srivastava

I live in the UAE — I'm surrounded by abaya stores. I see women shopping for them at every hour of the day. But when I looked online, those same women were being ignored. Major fashion platforms barely featured modest wear, and independent sellers were just pushing discounts on Instagram. No one was building anything real for them.

That bothered me. Abayas aren't a trend — they're culture. They're identity. And I kept thinking: what better place to bring AR technology into fashion than a market this deeply rooted, this underserved, and this ready? That's when I knew exactly what to build.
